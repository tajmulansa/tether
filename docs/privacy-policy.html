The short version

Ozmora has no accounts, no sign-up and no in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your phone. The only information that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising service needs to show and measure ads.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, gems, unlocked items and settings are saved on your phone only. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information ourselves. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics tools of our own.

Ads

Ozmora shows ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use:

  • your advertising ID
  • your IP address, and the approximate location that can be worked out from it
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version
  • how you interact with ads (for example views and taps)
  • diagnostic and crash information related to ads

This is used to show and measure ads, to personalise them where you have allowed it, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Ads are shown only on the loading screen and the game-over screen, and when you choose "Watch ad to revive".

Google's use of this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites ("How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services").

Your choices about ads

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions, Ozmora first shows a consent message. You can change your choice at any time under Settings, Privacy and ad choices (this entry appears where it applies to you).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with Google (AdMob) as described above, which acts under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

International transfers

Google may process information in countries other than your own. Google applies safeguards for such transfers as described in its policies.

Children

Ozmora is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

Data retention and deletion

We keep no personal data. Game data on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Data held by Google for ads is governed by Google's retention policies, and you can manage it in your Google account's ad settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, California and other US state laws, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are answered by the controls above and in your device settings. Contact us for anything else and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

The game runs offline from your device. No game data is transmitted by us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
