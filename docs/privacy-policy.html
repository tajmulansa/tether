Ozmora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Ozmora is a game made by Only Needed (Tajmul), India. This page explains what information the game uses.

Your game data

Your levels, stars, gems and settings are saved on your phone only. We never see them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Ads

Ozmora shows ads using Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information and how you interact with ads. Read how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where the law requires it (for example in Europe and the UK), the game asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings, Privacy and ad choices. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your Android settings.

What we do not do

We do not have accounts, collect your name, email or location, run our own analytics, or sell your data.

Children

Ozmora is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
