Ozmora Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Published by Only Needed (Tajmul), India

The short version

Ozmora has no accounts and no sign-up. Your progress stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is what the ad service needs to show ads, and what Google Play needs to process a purchase.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, gems, unlocked items and settings are saved on your phone only. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ads

Ozmora shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions, Ozmora first shows a consent message, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings, Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Typeface

The game may load its typeface from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when it does.

Children

Ozmora is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, California and other US state laws, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a regulator. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are answered by the controls above and in your device settings. Contact us for anything else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.
